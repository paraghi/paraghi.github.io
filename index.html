02 — Profile

A practitioner who teaches.

The most valuable lesson in finance is the one a textbook cannot offer — the lived experience of decisions made under uncertainty.

A Chartered Accountant by training, I spent twenty-five years in the corporate world, navigating financial strategy, capital decisions, and the day-to-day judgements that determine whether enterprises endure or unravel. That practice forms the spine of how I teach today.

For the past fifteen years, I have been a visiting faculty member at leading Indian business schools, where my courses explore the architecture of financial analysis — how to read a company, how to value it, and how to separate signal from noise in equity and mutual fund markets.

My approach is unapologetically applied. Cases are drawn from Indian markets. Frameworks are stress-tested against real filings. Students leave with not just models, but the instincts to question them.

India Inc. Q4 FY 2025-26 — The Year Mid Caps Outshone Everyone

Of the 5,069 listed companies on Indian exchanges, 643 had declared Q4 results by early May — and the picture that emerges is one of clear, broad-based recovery. Aggregate revenue grew 10.25% year-on-year, gross profit 12.87%, and net profit 17.43%. Q4 was decisively the strongest quarter of FY26, with 62% of reporting companies showing positive growth.

The headline story, however, belongs to mid caps. Net profit growth of 26.33% — nearly double large caps (12.9%) and almost triple small caps (9.59%) — reflects the operating leverage that emerges when discretionary consumption recovers and specialty sectors find pricing power. Lupin nearly doubled net profits on US generics recovery; BSE Ltd grew 64% as retail investor participation surged; Pidilite delivered 37% growth on premiumisation and input-cost moderation.

Large caps were anchored by IT services (Infosys +20.9%, TCS +12.1%) and improving banking metrics (HDFC Bank's gross NPA ratio compressed from 1.40% to 1.15% through the year). Reliance was the drag, with energy-margin compression pulling Q4 net profit down 8.1%. Among small caps, the story bifurcated sharply — niche players in diagnostics, wealth management, and specialty engineering grew 50-90%, while commodity-linked and regulated utility sectors weighed the aggregate down.
