01Methodology

The 7-Dimension Selection Framework.

Every fund in this report is evaluated against the same seven critical dimensions, with thresholds adapted to the risk-return profile of each category. Returns alone are noise; this framework isolates repeatable, skill-based outperformance.

1Consistent Alpha over Category AverageFunds beating benchmarks across 1Y, 3Y and 5Y demonstrate a repeatable process — not a single lucky window.
2Risk-Adjusted ReturnsSharpe & Sortino ratios test return quality. Target: Sharpe >0.70 (large-cap), >1.0 (mid & small-cap).
3Downside Capture RatioFunds that limit losses in falling markets recover faster — enabling superior long-term compounding.
4Fund Manager Tenure & StabilityLonger tenure reduces style drift. Prefer 5+ years (large-cap), 7+ years (small-cap).
5Assets Under ManagementOptimal ranges differ by cap: ₹1–50K Cr large, ₹3–15K Cr mid, ₹5–20K Cr small.
6Expense Ratio EfficiencyA 20–30 bps edge compounds to a 3–5% wealth advantage over twenty years. Cost is a near-certain return.
7Beta vs. Return QualityLow-beta funds (0.8–1.0) with high returns prove genuine stock-picking — not just market exposure.

The sweet spot of growth.

India's fastest-growing equity category, offering roughly 700 basis points of return premium over large-caps. Mid-cap stocks (ranked 101–250 by market cap) sit earlier in the business cycle, with earnings growth of 15–20% annually.

  • Superior alpha: top three funds beat the 21.1% category average by 100–150 bps annually.

  • Cost competitiveness: expense ratios of 0.42–0.74% sit 20–40 bps below the category average.

04Small-Cap Funds

The final frontier for wealth creation.

Small-cap funds are India's highest-compounding category. While 30% annual returns are achievable, 40% drawdowns are equally possible. Suitable only for investors with a 7–10 year commitment and high risk tolerance.

⚠ The Compounding Mismatch Small-caps trade at a 30–50% discount to large-cap multiples despite 25–35% annual EPS growth. This mismatch compounds: ₹10 lakh in a 25% CAGR small-cap fund becomes ₹1+ crore over 20 years — against ₹97.5 lakh in a 14% large-cap fund.
  • Superior long-term returns: the category averages 22–24% over 5Y, against 14% (large) and 21% (mid).
  • High volatility demands discipline: ~33% annual volatility requires a 7–10 year horizon and monthly SIP.
  • Cost discipline is critical: expense ratios of 0.42–0.75% sit below the 0.90%+ category average.
  • Manager skill is paramount: +4% to +8% annual alpha separates a 20% fund from a 40% one.

Five Lessons That Outlast Any Market Cycle

1
Cost Discipline Compounds

A 20–30 bps expense edge becomes a 3–5% wealth gain over twenty years. Favour the bottom quartile on cost.

2
Alpha is Repeatable but Rare

Only top-decile funds — roughly 5% of any category — consistently beat their benchmark. The seven dimensions exist to find them.

3
Risk Management Matters Most

Sharpe ratios, downside capture, and manager tenure predict long-term success far better than a single year's return.

4
Time Horizon Determines Category

Large-caps for 3–5 years, mid-caps for 5–7, small-caps for 7–10 minimum. Match the fund to the horizon, never the reverse.

5
SIP Discipline Beats Lump-Sum

Monthly investment smooths volatility and removes behavioural bias — most valuable in the mid and small-cap segments.
