10 Principles for the Indian Investor
01Start SIPs early and increase 10% annually. A ₹10,000/month SIP started at 25 at 13% CAGR grows to ₹6.3 crore by age 60. The same SIP started at 35 yields only ₹1.8 crore. Time in market beats timing the market — always.
02Hold 15% gold via Sovereign Gold Bonds — not physical. SGBs give 2.5% interest + capital gains tax-free at maturity. Rebalance when gold exceeds 20% of portfolio after a rally.
03Real estate = one primary home, then REITs. Direct investment in a second property is only rational if rental yield covers 60%+ of EMI in a top-6 city. REITs give commercial exposure without lock-in or transaction cost.
04Fixed deposits = emergency fund only. 3–6 months of expenses in a liquid FD is sensible. Beyond that, in the 30% tax bracket, FDs destroy real wealth. Especially damaging in a falling rate environment.
05Max your PPF every year without fail. ₹1.5 lakh/year at 7.1% tax-free, compounded over 15 years = ₹40.7 lakh. Zero tax, zero risk, government guarantee. The best fixed-income vehicle in India.
06Never exit equities during a crash — increase SIP instead. Every market correction is a buying opportunity. 83% of all 7-year Nifty holding periods since 1999 delivered above 10%. Panic-selling is the number one destroyer of wealth.
07Use the "100 minus age" rule for equity allocation. Age 30 → 70% equity. Age 50 → 50% equity. Age 65 → 35% equity. Reduce equity by 1% per year after 50.
08Rebalance once a year — not monthly. When any asset class deviates 5%+ from target weight, rebalance. Enforces disciplined buy-low/sell-high and controls drift.
09Prioritise tax efficiency. LTCG 12.5% on equity vs 30% on FD is a structural advantage. Hold equity 12+ months. Use SGB for gold. Max PPF and ELSS for 80C benefits every year.
10Don't chase last year's winner. In 2025, gold gave 74%. Equity gave 20% in 2024. Debt may outperform in 2026. A diversified allocation ensures you capture rotation without guessing the next winner.
